# Organisation logo sources

Downloaded on 1 November 2026. Logos identify the organisations in Adri's experience and education. They are third-party brand assets and are not original artwork by the portfolio owner. Logo colours and geometry are unchanged.

- `glints.svg`: original SVG embedded in the official Glints website, https://glints.com/ .
- `bukit-vista.png`: https://www.bukitvista.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/BukitVista-LOGO-ONLY-transparent-2.png , linked from the official Bukit Vista homepage, https://www.bukitvista.com/ .
- `monash.jpg`: official reverse-keyline logo for dark backgrounds, https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/image/0012/1738947/monash-university-stacked-reverse-keyline-shield.jpg . Brand reference: https://www.monash.edu/brandbook/brand-elements/our-logo .
